# Hello, world

Every good system starts with a `hello world`, so here's mine.

This site is a personal corner of the internet — part portfolio, part homelab
logbook. If you're a recruiter, the **resume** window and the **contact** links
are what you're after. If you're here because you also run *too many* services
on hardware in a closet, hit the terminal and poke around.

## Why a terminal?

Because I spend most of my day in one. The whole site reads from a single data
file, and the terminal and the windowed view are just two front-ends over the
same content. Type `help` to see what it knows.

## What's next

I'll use this space for short writeups — homelab builds, automation I'm proud
of, things that broke and how I fixed them. For now:
